# Weekly plan composer

Compose seven consecutive days of eating from the supplied `CANDIDATES` list, using the
exact same rules as the daily plan.

Additional rules for a week:

- Vary across days. No main dish more than twice in the week; rotate grains, pulses,
  vegetables and, where the diet allows, eggs, fish and meat.
- Keep the shopping practical: prefer ingredients that recur across several days so the
  grocery list is short, and put anything perishable early in the week.
- Respect the weekly rhythm given in the context — gym days, fasting days, eating out, the
  day they cook in bulk.
- Same prohibitions as always: only candidate `food_id` values, no nutrient numbers, no
  medication, no dose, no diagnosis.
